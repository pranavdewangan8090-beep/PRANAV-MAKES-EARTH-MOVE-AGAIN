The beam does push Earth away from the Sun — but the force on the arms is ~11× what they can take, so the arms break. Photon pushing at this power is not possible with these arms.
Reference: the Sun's whole output is L☉ = 3.828×1026 W; the notes' beam power is ≈ 1.8×104 × L☉, i.e. stored sunlight released as a pulse. Slide P below 6×1029 W and the arms survive.
