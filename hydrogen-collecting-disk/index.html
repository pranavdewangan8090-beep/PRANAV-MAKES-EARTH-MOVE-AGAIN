Calculations (from the notes)

H gas density1 m3 → 106 H atoms  (1 km3 → 1015)
net area (octa-hands)A = 8πR⊕2 = m2
relative speedΔv = 2.3×104 m/s = 23 km/s
swept volumeVol(t) = Δv · t · A = m3
atoms capturedN = n · Vol = atoms
mass capturedM = N · mH, mH = 1.67×10−27 kg → kg
kinetic energy / atom½ mH Δv2 = 4.4×10−19 J
